Our Story

Musafir Cafe started in 2022 with a simple idea, a place where travelers and locals could sit together over a good cup of coffee. The name "Musafir" means traveler, and that's exactly the vibe we wanted, warm, welcoming and a little nostalgic.

Every corner of our cafe is inspired by journeys, old maps on the walls, vintage suitcases as decor, and a menu that mixes flavors from different cultures.
